# Preparation: the loop, the people, the plan

Know the loop round by round, what each interviewer is grading, and how to prepare in two weeks.

<section class="chapter-context" markdown="1">

## Learn the shape of the conversation before the content

A CrowdStrike senior loop is decided in the design review, leveled in the hiring-manager call, and lost most often in recruiter silence. Each lesson below covers one round with the reported questions, what is being graded, and what to say. The ledger lesson labels every question found in public with its source, date, role, and weight, so you can judge it yourself.
