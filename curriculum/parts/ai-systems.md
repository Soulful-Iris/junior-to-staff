## Optional specialization after the core engineering path

An AI feature still needs an application contract, permission checks, budgets, observable failures, and a release decision. This specialization applies the earlier application, system-design, and production material to model-backed behavior.

Take it after the core journey when preparing for an AI product or platform role. It is not a prerequisite for conventional coding, backend, frontend, infrastructure, or system-design interviews.
