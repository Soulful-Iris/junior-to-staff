This source review supports 15 additional interview-practice pages. The source is a **live, user-submitted interview-question catalog** maintained by Hello Interview. Its entries say which companies users have associated with a prompt, but do not give the interview date for each report. It therefore supports “listed in a current community catalog,” not “asked in the past six months,” a frequency ranking, or a company's official question bank.

The linked problem pages create original practice contracts, assumptions, examples, diagrams, AWS mappings and follow-ups. Their company tags are evidence that candidates have associated a prompt type with those companies; they do not predict an individual interview. Readers should confirm the interview format with their recruiter.

## Why the practice set is broader than the reported prompts

The problems were selected to cover distinct interview design skills: product workflows and state ownership; high-write data systems; distributed coordination; observability/cardinality; isolation of untrusted work; and AWS service selection. This is a breadth choice, not a claim that these 15 are the most frequently asked prompts. The prior 21 practice pages remain in the same learning sequence.

**Research limitation:** Catalog entries were reviewed on the date above, but a current page or recently crawled result does not date the interview anecdote. We found no reliable public source exposing recent report dates across this full list. Any claim that all 15 were asked within the last 6 or 12 months would exceed the evidence.
