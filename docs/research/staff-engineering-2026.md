Dropbox's IC5 framework describes multi-team direction and organizational impact. That is evidence about one employer's framework, not a universal senior/staff boundary or proof that coding expectations cease rising. StaffEng's Tech Lead, Architect, Solver and Right Hand categories are useful descriptive vocabulary, not an exhaustive taxonomy or a staffing-ratio formula. Neither undated page counts as a recent interview event.

## Curriculum choices

We ask a senior candidate to own a coherent technical decision and its failure behavior; the lead extension asks who coordinates the cross-team constraint, migration, operating budget and recovery authority. Practice producing a short design with alternatives, a migration plan that reaches completion, and a record linking decisions to measured consequences. Those deliverables make the intended skill observable; they do not prove a promotion outcome.

A promotion packet, sponsorship and glue work may matter in a specific organization. Verify the target employer's current framework and manager expectations rather than turning a personal narrative into a mandatory recipe. Our [interview ledger](interview-evidence.md) explicitly retains gaps for current named-company lead loops and does not fill them with engineering blogs.
